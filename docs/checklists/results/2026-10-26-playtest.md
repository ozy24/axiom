## HoonyMode (2026-10-27, build `eb055209`)

Chris: "once a player has won a round they should be able to move around the
map (only loser goes into spec) until the round restarts. There should be a
pause of a second or two showing the death/kill message, then players should
respawn but not be able to move for the 5 second countdown, then the next
round starts." Also: "the bot model disappeared on the second round."

Outcome: the round flow is now ROUND OVER (2 s, `match_round_pause`: winner
free, the dead stay dead) -> respawn, frozen, "Next round in 5..1"
(`match_round_countdown` 5) -> FIGHT!. The vanishing bot was the old hold
writing `svflags = SVF_NOCLIENT`, which wiped `SVF_BOT`; nobody is hidden now.
Harness tier 7 now needs a second frag-ended round (bots still playing after
a reset).

Also: "when a player suicides it takes a frag away from their score where it
should be giving the frag to the other player." Outcome: `match_suicide_point`
(1 in `hoony`): a suicide or death to the world is +1 to the only opponent.
Checked live: Bob died in slime (Alice +1, the round ended), Alice's `kill` in
the pause (Bob +1).
